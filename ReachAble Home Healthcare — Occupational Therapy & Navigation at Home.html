Two kinds of help. One visit.

Occupational Therapy

Regulated clinical care, delivered where you live.

  • Preventative home visits, before an issue arises
  • Home safety & fall-risk assessment
  • Equipment prescription (mobility, bathroom safety, ADP)
  • Functional assessment
  • Caregiver training and coaching
  • Reports for insurers and care applications

Service Navigation

Making sense of a system that isn't built to be simple.

  • Source of support to help navigate the health care system
  • Coordinating with the healthcare team, such as doctors, PSWs, care coordinators, and family
  • Ongoing support for complex situations

Older adults staying in their own home Families planning ahead, before a crisis hits Family caregivers feeling overwhelmed People recovering from a fall or injury People managing chronic conditions at home Families facing a long-term-care decision
